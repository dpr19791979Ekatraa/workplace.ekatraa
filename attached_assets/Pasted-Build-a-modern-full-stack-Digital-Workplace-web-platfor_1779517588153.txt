Build a modern full-stack “Digital Workplace” web platform for my company where all team members can securely access, create, upload, organize, and collaborate on work-related resources such as forms, spreadsheets, presentations, documents, tasks, media files, links, announcements, and internal tools — similar to a combination of Google Workspace, Notion, Slack, and an Admin ERP panel.

The platform should be scalable, mobile responsive, secure, fast, and designed for real business operations.

========================================
CORE PLATFORM GOAL
==================

Create a centralized company workspace where:

* Employees can log in and access company resources.
* Teams can collaborate on projects.
* Admins can manage employees, permissions, storage, tasks, and company operations.
* Files and tools are organized department-wise and project-wise.
* Everything works from one dashboard.
